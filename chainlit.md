# Cravio

Your food concierge for Swiggy Food and Instamart.

- Ask for food or groceries: "Suggest a veg dinner under ₹300, like what I usually get."
- Every cart change waits for your approval. Cravio never places or pays for an order.
- Ask about an order or report a problem: "Where is my order?", "My last order had a missing item."
